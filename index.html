Before you reserve

Common questions

How much is the reservation fee?

₱30,000 for studios and ₱60,000 for 1-bedroom units at both projects. The reservation fee is non-refundable and non-transferable, so reserve once you've chosen your unit.

Are the prices all-in?

The total contract prices on the developer's price lists include miscellaneous charges and VAT. Prices differ per floor and view, so ask me for the exact price of the unit you like.

Can I pay from abroad?

Yes. All transactions are processed in Philippine pesos, and any foreign currency amount is an estimate only. Message me and I'll walk you through the payment arrangements for buyers abroad.

When is the turnover?

Kizuna Heights is near RFO with a projected turnover in Q1 2027, per the November 2026 price list. Plumeria Heights is pre-selling with a target turnover in 2028, per the developer's brochure. Schedules may change, so ask me for the latest.

Who do I pay?

All payments for both projects are made payable to Vista Residences Inc., the developer named on the official price lists and computations.

Can I visit the site?

Yes. The developer asks that property previews be booked at least two days in advance so the site is ready for viewing. Request a site viewing below and I'll confirm the schedule with you.

Which one is better for a student?

Kizuna Heights is 350 m from De La Salle University and near RFO, so it suits a student starting soon. Plumeria Heights is beside LRT-1 Quirino with a lower entry price, so it suits families planning a few years ahead.

Are the units furnished?

The interior photos are artist's perspectives with furniture for display only. Ask me what comes with the unit you like.
